Privacy Policy

Effective 5 July 2026

In short: this is a static site with no contact form and no advertising. It uses privacy-conscious, anonymous analytics to understand how the site is used, does not sell your data, and handles personal details only when you book a call or email.

Who Runs This Site

This site, muhzee.com, is operated by Muhammad Zeeshan, who works as an independent Fractional AI Lead based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. This policy explains how information from visitors and prospective clients is handled.

What Is Collected

The site itself does not run a contact form and does not ask you to create an account. Information reaches me only through the channels you choose to use. When you book a call through the scheduling link, you provide your name, email address, and any details you enter in the booking. When you email me@muhzee.com, I receive your email address and the content of your message. If you connect on LinkedIn, that interaction is governed by LinkedIn.

How It Is Used

Information you send is used only to respond to your enquiry, to schedule and hold calls, and to carry out any work you later engage me for. It is not sold, rented, or used for advertising. It is kept only as long as needed for our correspondence and any resulting engagement, and for records I am required to keep.

Third Party Services

Three services support this site. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests them from Google and Google receives your IP address in order to serve them. Call bookings run on Google Calendar appointment scheduling, so Google processes the booking details on my behalf. Basic analytics are provided by PostHog, hosted in the European Union, to understand which pages and links visitors use; this relies on cookies or similar local storage and records information such as approximate location, device, and browser. The analytics are anonymous, are not used for advertising, and do not identify you personally. Each service is covered by its provider's own privacy terms.

Your Choices

You can contact me at any time to ask what information I hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted, subject to any records I am legally required to retain. This policy is governed by the laws of the United Arab Emirates.

Questions about this policy go to me@muhzee.com.
