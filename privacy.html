Privacy Policy

Effective 5 July 2026

In short: this is a static site with no contact form, no advertising, and no analytics tracking. It does not sell your data. The only information handled is what you send when you book a call or email.

Who Runs This Site

This site, muhzee.com, is operated by Muhammad Zeeshan, who runs an AI systems consulting practice based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. This policy explains how information from visitors and prospective clients is handled.

What Is Collected

The site itself does not run a contact form and does not ask you to create an account. Information reaches me only through the channels you choose to use. When you book a call through the scheduling link, you provide your name, email address, and any details you enter in the booking. When you email me@muhzee.com, I receive your email address and the content of your message. If you connect on LinkedIn, that interaction is governed by LinkedIn.

How It Is Used

Information you send is used only to respond to your enquiry, to schedule and hold calls, and to carry out any work you later engage me for. It is not sold, rented, or used for advertising. It is kept only as long as needed for our correspondence and any resulting engagement, and for records I am required to keep.

Third Party Services

Two services support this site. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests them from Google and Google receives your IP address in order to serve them. Call bookings run on Google Calendar appointment scheduling, so Google processes the booking details on my behalf. Both are covered by Google's own privacy terms. The site sets no advertising or analytics cookies of its own.

Your Choices

You can contact me at any time to ask what information I hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted, subject to any records I am legally required to retain. This policy is governed by the laws of the United Arab Emirates.
